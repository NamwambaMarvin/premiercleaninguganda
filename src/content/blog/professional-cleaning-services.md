### Office and Commercial Cleaning

A clean office creates a better first impression for clients and supports a healthier workspace for employees. Our commercial cleaning services can include:

- Desk and workstation cleaning  
- Restroom cleaning and restocking  
- Kitchen and breakroom cleaning  
- Floor care and carpet maintenance  
- High-touch surface sanitizing  
- Waste collection and bin cleaning  

We work around your operating hours to avoid disrupting your business.

### Deep Cleaning Services

Routine cleaning keeps a space presentable, but deep cleaning removes hidden dirt, grease, grime, and buildup. This is ideal for homes, offices, restaurants, schools, and commercial premises.

Deep cleaning may cover:

- Tile, grout, and hard-floor cleaning  
- Kitchen degreasing  
- Bathroom descaling and sanitizing  
- Carpet and upholstery cleaning  
- Ceiling, vent, and hard-to-reach dusting  
- Window and glass cleaning  

### Move-In and Move-Out Cleaning

Moving is stressful enough without worrying about cleaning. Our move-in and move-out cleaning service prepares a property for new occupants or helps tenants leave it in excellent condition.

This service can include appliance interiors, cabinets, drawers, walls, floors, bathrooms, kitchens, and storage areas.

### Post-Construction Cleaning

After renovation or construction, dust and debris can settle across every surface. Post-construction cleaning removes fine dust, paint marks, adhesive residue, and construction waste so the space is ready to use.
