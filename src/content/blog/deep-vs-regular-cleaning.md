## Understanding the Difference

Regular cleaning keeps a space tidy day to day. Deep cleaning goes further, targeting dirt, grease, grime, and buildup in areas that are often missed during routine cleaning.

Both services are important. Regular cleaning maintains your space, while deep cleaning restores it.

Premier Cleaning Uganda offers both routine maintenance and specialized cleaning packages for homes, businesses, and industrial facilities.

## What Regular Cleaning Includes

Regular cleaning is ideal for maintaining a clean environment between deep cleans. It commonly includes:

- Sweeping, mopping, and vacuuming  
- Dusting furniture and surfaces  
- Cleaning bathrooms and kitchens  
- Emptying bins  
- Tidying common areas  
- Wiping countertops and visible surfaces  
- Basic restroom sanitation  

For homes, this may be weekly or biweekly. For offices, it is often daily or several times per week.

## What Deep Cleaning Includes

Deep cleaning is more detailed and time-intensive. It is designed to remove accumulated dirt and refresh areas that regular cleaning cannot fully address.

A deep clean may include:

- Degreasing kitchen surfaces, cookers, and extractors  
- Descaling bathroom tiles, showers, sinks, and toilets  
- Scrubbing tile grout and hard floors  
- Cleaning behind and beneath furniture and appliances  
- Washing windows, frames, and glass partitions  
- Cleaning vents, ceilings, and hard-to-reach areas  
- Carpet and upholstery cleaning  
- Sanitizing high-touch surfaces  
- Post-construction dust and debris removal  

## When to Choose Regular Cleaning

Choose regular cleaning if:

- Your space is already in good condition  
- You want to maintain cleanliness consistently  
- You have a busy household or office  
- You need scheduled cleaning support  
- You want to prevent dirt, dust, and clutter from building up  

## When to Choose Deep Cleaning

Choose deep cleaning if:

- You are moving into a new home or office  
- You are preparing a property for new tenants  
- You are hosting an event or expecting guests  
- Your space has not been cleaned professionally in a long time  
- There is visible grease, mold, grime, dust, or staining  
- You have completed renovation or construction work  
- You need a seasonal or end-of-year clean  

## How Often Should You Deep Clean?

There is no single rule for every property, but a practical schedule is:

| Property type | Regular cleaning | Deep cleaning |
|---|---|---|
| Home | Weekly or biweekly | Every 3–6 months |
| Office | Daily or several times weekly | Every 3–6 months |
| Restaurant or kitchen | Daily | Monthly or quarterly |
| Retail shop | Daily or several times weekly | Quarterly |
| Post-construction site | As needed | Immediately after works |

The right schedule depends on foot traffic, location, weather, and the type of activities in the space.

## Why Deep Cleaning Is Worth It

Deep cleaning improves appearance, hygiene, and comfort. It also helps identify maintenance issues early, such as leaking pipes, mold-prone areas, damaged grout, or blocked drainage.

For businesses, a deep clean can prepare a facility for inspections, client visits, reopening, or a change of season.
