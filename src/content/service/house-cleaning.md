## Our House Cleaning Services

### General Home Cleaning

Our standard house cleaning service keeps your home neat and welcoming. We dust surfaces, vacuum carpets, sweep and mop floors, clean mirrors, empty bins, tidy living spaces, and wipe commonly touched areas.

### Deep Cleaning

Ideal for a first clean, post-event cleaning, seasonal cleaning, or when your home needs extra attention. Deep cleaning covers hard-to-reach areas, accumulated dirt, grease, bathroom buildup, and detailed kitchen cleaning.

### Kitchen Cleaning

We clean countertops, sinks, cabinet exteriors, stovetops, appliance surfaces, dining areas, and floors. This helps remove grease, food residue, and everyday kitchen mess.

### Bathroom Cleaning

Our team scrubs and sanitizes toilets, showers, bathtubs, sinks, tiles, mirrors, and bathroom fittings, leaving your bathroom clean, fresh, and hygienic.

### Bedroom and Living Area Cleaning

We dust furniture, tidy spaces, make beds on request, vacuum carpets and rugs, clean floors, and remove cobwebs to create a comfortable living environment.

### Move-In and Move-Out Cleaning

Moving home? We provide thorough move-in and move-out cleaning to prepare a new home for occupancy or leave your previous residence clean for handover.

### Post-Construction Cleaning

After renovation or construction, we remove dust, debris, paint marks where possible, and construction residue so your home is ready to live in.
