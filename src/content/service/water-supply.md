## Why water supply planning matters

Water is essential for drinking, cooking, cleaning, sanitation, and business operations. When the main supply stops, properties without storage or an alternative supply can face interrupted work, hygiene challenges, and inconvenience for residents, staff, or customers.

A planned water supply solution helps you maintain essential services during an outage. It can also reduce the stress of last-minute arrangements and help your property continue operating with minimal disruption.

The right solution depends on the size of the property, daily water use, storage capacity, access for delivery, and how long you need supply to last.

## What does a water supply service include?

A water supply service during an outage may include the delivery, storage, or distribution of clean water to your property. Depending on your needs, a service may include:

- Water delivery by tanker or bowser
- Filling storage tanks, drums, or other approved containers
- Supplying water for domestic, commercial, or construction use
- Pump installation or support for moving water from storage to outlets
- Checking tank access points, pipes, valves, and connections
- Emergency or scheduled water delivery during an outage
- Advice on storage capacity and water-use planning

A clear service plan helps ensure water reaches the right storage point safely and that your property has enough supply for its needs.

## How much water should you store?

There is no single storage target for every property. Your needs depend on the number of people, type of activities, and whether water is needed for drinking, sanitation, cleaning, or business operations.

- **Small households:** May benefit from a storage tank sized for several days of essential use.
- **Offices and commercial premises:** Should consider storage for sanitation, cleaning, staff needs, and customer-facing facilities.
- **Schools, apartments, and institutions:** May need larger storage capacity and a clear plan for priority areas.
- **Construction sites and events:** Often require scheduled deliveries or larger temporary storage arrangements.

Plan for essential needs first, including drinking water, handwashing, toilets, cleaning, and any critical business processes. Keep a buffer for unexpected delays in restoration or delivery.

## Water supply tips during an outage

### Store water safely

Use clean, covered, food-safe containers or approved storage tanks. Keep storage areas clean and protect water from dust, insects, and contamination.

### Prioritise essential uses

During an outage, prioritise drinking water, sanitation, handwashing, and critical cleaning needs. Reduce non-essential water use until normal supply resumes.

### Check storage and access points

Ensure tanks, taps, pipes, and access points are in good condition before an outage occurs. A tank that is difficult to fill or a blocked outlet can create delays when water is needed most.

### Arrange delivery before storage runs out

Do not wait until tanks are completely empty. Monitoring your usage and arranging delivery early can help avoid interruptions, especially during extended outages or high-demand periods.

### Use pumps and fittings appropriately

If water must be moved from a tank to bathrooms, kitchens, or work areas, ensure pumps, hoses, and fittings are suitable for the job. Avoid improvised connections that may leak, contaminate water, or create safety risks.

## How to choose a water supply service in Uganda

Before requesting a quotation, provide clear details about your property and water needs. This makes it easier for a provider to recommend the right delivery volume, schedule, and equipment.

### Include these details in your quotation request:

- Property type: home, office, school, hotel, apartment, or construction site
- Location and accessibility for a delivery vehicle
- Estimated number of people using the property
- Daily or weekly water requirement
- Storage tank size and current water level
- Whether water is needed for drinking, sanitation, cleaning, or operations
- Preferred delivery schedule or emergency response needs
- Any pump, hose, or distribution requirements

Ask the provider to confirm the water volume, delivery method, timeline, and total cost. Comparing quotations based on the same water volume and service scope makes it easier to choose the right option.

Yes. Water supply services can support offices, apartments, schools, hotels, restaurants, factories, and construction sites. Share your estimated daily usage and preferred delivery schedule when requesting a quotation.

Include storage tanks, filling points, pumps, pipes, taps, toilets, kitchens, handwashing areas, and critical work areas. Also record current water levels, estimated usage, and any access restrictions for delivery vehicles.
