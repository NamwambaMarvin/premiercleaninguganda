Terrazzo floors are durable, attractive, and common in homes, offices, hotels, schools, and commercial spaces across Uganda. With the right cleaning routine, terrazzo can stay smooth, glossy, and presentable while resisting everyday wear.

Whether you care for a small residential floor or a large commercial space, this guide explains what terrazzo cleaning involves, how often to clean it, and what to consider when choosing a professional cleaning service.

## What does terrazzo cleaning include?

Terrazzo cleaning covers the regular care of floors made from a mixture of stone chips, such as marble or granite, held together with cement or epoxy. Depending on the floor condition and service plan, cleaning may include:

- Sweeping, dust mopping, and vacuuming loose dirt
- Mopping with a suitable pH-neutral cleaning solution
- Spot-cleaning scuff marks, spills, and stubborn stains
- Machine scrubbing for larger or heavily used areas
- Rinsing and drying the floor to prevent residue
- Polishing or buffing to restore shine
- Grinding and refinishing for scratched, dull, or damaged terrazzo
- Applying or renewing a protective sealer where appropriate

A clear cleaning checklist helps protect the floor’s finish while keeping high-traffic areas clean and safe.

## Why terrazzo cleaning matters

Terrazzo is hard-wearing, but dirt, grit, spills, and foot traffic can gradually dull its surface. Regular cleaning removes abrasive particles before they scratch the floor and helps maintain a clean, professional appearance.

In entrances, corridors, reception areas, and other busy spaces, dust and moisture can also create slippery conditions. Consistent cleaning helps keep walkways presentable and reduces the buildup of grime in grout lines, edges, and corners.

The right approach depends on whether the terrazzo is cement-based or epoxy-based, whether it has been sealed, and the level of traffic it receives.

## How often should terrazzo be cleaned?

There is no single schedule for every floor. Use the amount of foot traffic, type of business, and visible condition of the surface to set a practical plan.

- **Daily:** Dust mop or vacuum entrances, corridors, reception areas, and other high-traffic spaces.
- **Daily or several times a week:** Mop floors using a pH-neutral cleaner, depending on traffic and spills.
- **Weekly:** Clean edges, corners, skirting areas, and less-visible sections more thoroughly.
- **Monthly:** Carry out a deeper scrub or machine cleaning where needed.
- **Periodically:** Arrange polishing, grinding, or resealing if the floor looks dull, scratched, stained, or worn.

Review the schedule when occupancy, weather, construction activity, or foot traffic changes.

## Terrazzo cleaning tips for Ugandan properties

### Use pH-neutral cleaning products

Avoid acidic cleaners, bleach, ammonia-based products, abrasive powders, and harsh degreasers. These can dull the surface, damage the finish, or degrade a sealer. A pH-neutral stone and tile cleaner, diluted according to the product instructions, is generally the safer option.

### Remove grit before mopping

Dust mopping or vacuuming first helps remove grit that could scratch the terrazzo during mopping. Pay extra attention to entrances, where shoes bring in soil, sand, and moisture.

### Clean spills promptly

Wipe up water, oil, food, and coloured liquids as soon as possible. Leaving spills to dry can make stains harder to remove and may affect the appearance of unsealed or cement-based terrazzo.

### Avoid excessive water

Use a damp mop rather than flooding the floor. Excess water can leave residue, create slip hazards, and seep into joints or unsealed areas.

### Do not use harsh scrubbing pads

Aggressive pads, steel wool, and abrasive brushes can scratch or dull terrazzo. Use soft microfiber mops, soft-bristled brushes, or equipment and pads suitable for the floor’s finish.

## How to choose terrazzo cleaning services in Uganda

Before hiring a cleaning company, ask for a written service plan. It should explain the cleaning method, products to be used, equipment, drying arrangements, and how polishing or repairs will be handled.

### Consider these questions:

- What type of terrazzo does the provider clean: cement-based, epoxy-based, polished, or sealed?
- Are pH-neutral products and non-abrasive methods included?
- Will the service cover edges, corners, stairs, and hard-to-reach areas?
- Can the provider polish, buff, grind, or restore dull terrazzo if needed?
- How will furniture, walls, electrical outlets, and nearby surfaces be protected?
- What safety measures are used during machine cleaning and polishing?
- Will the team report cracks, loose chips, or areas that need specialist repair?

Compare service details and methods, not just price. A plan that matches your floor type and level of use makes it easier to maintain consistent results.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does terrazzo cleaning cost in Uganda?

Costs vary by floor size, condition, level of soiling, location, and whether polishing, grinding, or sealing is included. Request an itemized quote so you can compare similar services and understand what is covered.

### Can I use ordinary floor cleaner on terrazzo?

Not always. Many general-purpose cleaners are too acidic, alkaline, or abrasive for terrazzo. Use a pH-neutral cleaner formulated for stone, tile, or terrazzo surfaces and follow the manufacturer’s instructions.

### Does terrazzo need to be sealed?

Cement-based terrazzo is porous and may benefit from a suitable penetrating or water-based sealer, while some epoxy terrazzo surfaces may not require sealing. The correct answer depends on the floor’s construction, finish, and manufacturer or installer guidance.

### How can I restore shine to dull terrazzo?

Regular buffing or polishing can improve appearance, while deeply scratched or worn terrazzo may need professional grinding and refinishing. Avoid trying to restore the surface with harsh chemicals or abrasive pads.

### What should I include in a terrazzo cleaning checklist?

Include entrances, corridors, reception areas, stairs, edges, corners, skirting, drains, and high-traffic walkways. Also record stains, cracks, loose chips, dull patches, and areas where the sealer may need attention.

## Keep your terrazzo floors clean and glossy

A practical terrazzo cleaning plan starts with regular dust removal, pH-neutral cleaning, prompt spill response, and periodic polishing or sealing. Set clear expectations with your cleaning provider, choose a schedule that suits your property, and review the floor’s condition regularly.

Consistent care helps terrazzo floors stay attractive, safer underfoot, and easier to maintain over time.
